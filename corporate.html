Frequently Asked Questions

Corporate Account FAQ

What is required to set up NET-30 corporate billing?
Qualified corporate entities completing our application form receive credit approval within 24 hours. NET-30 terms apply to all approved corporate accounts with monthly invoicing.
Can we set spending caps or vehicle restrictions per employee?
Yes, travel managers can set custom booking permissions inside our portal — restricting vehicle classes or setting per-trip spending limits according to your corporate travel policy.
How are roadshows with multiple stops managed?
Our dedicated roadshow desk reviews your full itinerary, assigns primary and backup chauffeurs, and maintains direct phone/SMS dispatch contact with your executive assistant throughout the day.
