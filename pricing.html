Frequently Asked Questions

Pricing FAQ

Are driver gratuity and tips included?
Yes, standard service gratuity is included in your upfront quote. Additional tipping is purely optional at your discretion.
Are there surge prices during high-demand hours?
No. Unlike ride-hailing apps, Chauffeur strictly operates on fixed, transparent pricing regardless of weather, rush hour, or special events.
How are additional waiting hours billed for hourly service?
Hourly service is billed in 30-minute increments beyond your initial booked duration at your agreed hourly rate.
