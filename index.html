Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our executive booking and chauffeur operations.

What happens if my flight is delayed?
We monitor all commercial and private flights in real time using flight radar tracking. Your chauffeur's dispatch time automatically adjusts to your actual landing time, guaranteeing your driver will be waiting when you land.
How much complimentary waiting time is included?
All airport pickups include 60 minutes of complimentary wait time from the moment your plane touches down. Non-airport pickups include 15 minutes of complimentary wait time.
Can I book an hourly chauffeur for multiple stops?
Yes, our Hourly As-Directed service allows you to keep a vehicle and dedicated driver at your disposal for as long as required, with unlimited stops within your designated service area.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations made up to 2 hours prior to pickup time for standard sedans and SUVs receive a 100% refund. For executive vans and specialized event fleets, 24 hours notice is requested.
