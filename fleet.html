Frequently Asked Questions

Fleet FAQ

What year models are in your fleet?
All vehicles in our executive fleet are strictly current or late-model vehicles under 3 years old, ensuring maximum safety, reliability, and modern interior comforts.
Can I specify a specific vehicle model when booking?
Yes, you can select your preferred vehicle class (e.g. First Class Sedan, Executive SUV, Sprinter Van) during reservation. Specific vehicle colors (all black fleet) are guaranteed.
What is the luggage capacity for sedan vs SUV?
Executive sedans comfortably hold 2 large suitcases plus 2 carry-on bags. Executive SUVs hold up to 6 large suitcases and 4 carry-ons. For larger groups with heavy luggage, our Executive Van is recommended.
